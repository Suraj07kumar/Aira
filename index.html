How It Works

Aira feels what
you breathe

PM2.5 particles are so tiny they pass straight into your lungs — the same air that makes Aira wilt makes you sick too. Airveda monitors help you see it before you feel it.

🌿
Sensors monitor air
Airveda devices measure PM2.5, PM10, CO₂, temperature and humidity in real time, indoors and outdoors.
01
📊
AQI is calculated
Readings are processed on the cloud to generate an AQI score from 0–500 and mapped to a health category.
02
💜
Aira reflects it
Aira's mood, posture, flowers and sky colour all shift to match the AQI — so you feel the air quality intuitively.
03
🔔
You get alerted
The Airveda app sends you health alerts when air quality drops, so you can act: close windows, wear a mask, stay in.
04
